# Privacy policy

Cibim is a personal money tracker for Android. Last updated 1 November 2026.

## Your data stays on your phone

Everything you enter (entries, accounts, categories, people, photos and settings) is stored on your phone. The app has no account to create and no server of its own. It has no ads and no analytics, and it sends nothing to its developer.

## Google Drive backup

This is off until you turn it on. When it's on, the app saves a copy of your data to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, once a night and when you ask it to.

- The app asks Google only for the `drive.appdata` permission. It can see the folder it created for itself, and nothing else in your Drive.
- The copy goes straight from your phone to your Google account. Nobody else receives it.
- You can restore it to the app after a reinstall or on a new phone.
- To delete it, turn backup off, then remove the app under Manage apps in Google Drive's settings.

## Bank notifications

This is off until you turn it on and give the permission in Android's settings. The app then reads notifications only from the apps you pick, on your phone, to offer ready-made entries. For other apps it keeps only their package name, so you can pick them. Notifications are never sent anywhere. Entries you confirm are saved like any others, so they're included in your backup.

## Adding by voice

The microphone is used only while you add an entry by voice, after you've allowed it. Android's own speech recognition, the one your phone's keyboard uses, turns what you say into text. Depending on the phone and the language, that happens on the phone or with Google's recognition service. The app keeps only the text, to fill in the entry you then check and save. No recording is kept.

## Files you choose

Export CSV saves a file where you tell it to. Importing a backup from another app reads the file you pick, on your phone.

## Sharing

Your data is never sold, or shared with anyone.
